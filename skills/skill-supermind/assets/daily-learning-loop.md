## 3. Selective study (20–30 min)

- Mechanism in my own words:
- One worked example:

## 4. Connection map (10 min)

- Prior idea this builds on:
- Contrasting idea:
- Related skill or tool:
- Open question:

## 5. Closed-book retrieval (10 min)

Close the source. Write or speak:

1. The idea in one sentence:
2. The mechanism from memory:
3. The example from memory:
4. A new prediction:
5. A question I still cannot answer:

Then reopen the source and mark only what was missing.

## 6. Compress (5 min)

Five lines maximum:

- Claim:
- Mechanism:
- Example:
- Boundary:
- Next use:

## Schedule

- Review 1: tomorrow
- Review 2: in 3 days
- Review 3: in 1 week
- Review 4: in 3–4 weeks

Adjust to difficulty. If retrieval is easy, increase delay; if consistently weak, return to the missing mechanism rather than rereading everything.
